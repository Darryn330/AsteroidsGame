Asteroid Game

A program demonstrating how 5 of the variables written in the Floater class work.
Pressing 'w' will cause the ship to accelerate forwards.
Pressing 'a' or 'd' turns the ship left and right.
Pressing 's' decelerates the ship.
Pressing 'h' causes hyperspace, putting the ship into a random position and facing a random direction.
Pressing 'b' causes an even faster acceleration, something I'd call a boost.
